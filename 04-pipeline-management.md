# Pipeline Management

## Recommended opportunity stages

| Stage | Required evidence | Next action |
|---|---|---|
| Discovery scheduled | Meeting booked with relevant stakeholder | Confirm agenda |
| Discovery completed | Pain point, need, and context documented | Agree solution review |
| Qualified opportunity | Fit, need, authority path, timing, and next step confirmed | Prepare demo/proposal |
| Solution presented | Relevant solution demonstrated | Resolve questions |
| Proposal shared | Commercial proposal delivered | Schedule decision follow-up |
| Negotiation / approval | Commercial or approval process active | Track agreed actions |
| Closed won | Signed agreement/confirmed purchase | Onboarding handoff |
| Closed lost | Outcome and reason recorded | Recycle only when appropriate |

## Pipeline rules

- Every open opportunity must have an owner, value/range, next step, and next-action date.
- Stage changes require the evidence listed above.
- Automated reminders flag overdue next actions and stalled opportunities.
- Forecast categories remain separate from pipeline stages.
- Closed-lost reasons use a controlled list plus optional notes.
- A weekly pipeline review focuses on movement, risk, next action, and support needed.

## Useful automations

- Create follow-up tasks after discovery, demos, and proposals.
- Alert owners when an opportunity has no activity for a defined period.
- Notify managers when high-value opportunities stall.
- Trigger onboarding tasks after closed won.
- Remove active opportunities and customers from acquisition campaigns.
