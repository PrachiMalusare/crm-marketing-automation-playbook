# Marketing-to-Sales Handoff SLA

## Handoff package

Sales receives:

- contact, company, role, and territory;
- original and latest source;
- expressed product/use-case interest;
- score, threshold, and contributing signals;
- recent high-intent activities;
- consent/communication status; and
- recommended next action.

## Service levels

| Priority | Example | First-action target |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | Demo/contact-sales request | Within 1 business hour |
| P2 | High-fit MQL with strong intent | Within 4 business hours |
| P3 | Standard MQL | Within 1 business day |

Targets are illustrative and should match team capacity and operating hours.

## Sales disposition

Within the SLA, sales selects one outcome:

- **Accepted:** qualification begins.
- **Recycle:** relevant but not ready; reason and review date required.
- **Disqualified:** not a valid opportunity; standardized reason required.
- **Duplicate/existing:** merge or associate with the correct record.

Marketing reviews rejection patterns weekly and updates targeting, forms, content, or scoring when evidence supports a change.

## Handoff QA checklist

- Correct owner and territory
- No duplicate active opportunity
- Required context populated
- Scoring reason visible
- Task and due date created
- Customer and consent suppressions applied
- Escalation alert tested
