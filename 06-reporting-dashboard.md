# Reporting Dashboard Framework

## Executive view

- Leads, MQLs, SQLs, opportunities, and customers by period
- Marketing-sourced and marketing-influenced pipeline
- Funnel conversion rates
- Average time between lifecycle stages
- Pipeline value and closed-won revenue by source

## Operational view

- MQL acceptance and rejection rates
- First-action SLA compliance
- Leads without owners
- Stalled opportunities and overdue tasks
- Workflow errors, bounces, unsubscribes, and duplicates
- Nurture engagement and conversion by sequence

## Core formulas

- **MQL conversion rate** = MQLs ÷ leads × 100
- **Sales acceptance rate** = accepted MQLs ÷ reviewed MQLs × 100
- **Opportunity conversion rate** = opportunities ÷ SQLs × 100
- **Win rate** = closed-won opportunities ÷ closed opportunities × 100
- **Lead velocity** = (current-period qualified leads − prior-period qualified leads) ÷ prior-period qualified leads × 100

## Reporting discipline

- State whether attribution is first-touch, last-touch, or multi-touch.
- Separate volume from quality and revenue impact.
- Compare channels using consistent date and lifecycle definitions.
- Annotate campaign launches and major process changes.
- Use trends and cohorts rather than interpreting small samples as proof.
