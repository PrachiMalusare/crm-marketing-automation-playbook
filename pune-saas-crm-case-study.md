# Illustrative Case Study: Pune B2B SaaS CRM Engine

> This is a hypothetical portfolio scenario. The organization, baseline, targets, and outcomes are illustrative—not client claims.

## Scenario

A Pune-based B2B SaaS provider sells CRM, ERP, and AI chatbot solutions to growing manufacturing, industrial, and service businesses. Leads arrive through SEO pages, LinkedIn outreach, referrals, events, and website forms, but follow-up is inconsistent and source data is incomplete.

## Objective

Design a HubSpot- or Zoho-based system that captures demand, identifies suitable accounts, nurtures prospects, and hands sales-ready leads to the correct owner with complete context.

## Ideal customer signals

- Pune or nearby industrial/technology clusters
- 25–500 employees
- Manual or fragmented lead/customer processes
- Need for CRM visibility, ERP integration, or automated support
- Operations, sales, service, IT, or founder-level stakeholder

## Proposed solution

### 1. Capture and segmentation

- Create solution-specific forms and landing pages.
- Standardize industry, company size, location, use case, and source.
- Use progressive profiling to reduce initial form friction.
- Associate contacts with company records and preserve original source.

### 2. Qualification and routing

- Combine ICP fit with meaningful engagement.
- Route CRM, ERP, and chatbot interests to relevant specialists.
- Prioritize direct consultation/demo requests.
- Create an owner task and notify sales with the scoring explanation.

### 3. Automated nurture

- **CRM track:** pipeline visibility, follow-up, and reporting use cases.
- **ERP track:** inventory, operations, and integration use cases.
- **AI chatbot track:** qualification, support, multilingual Q&A, and escalation use cases.
- Exit nurture when a lead replies, books a meeting, opts out, or enters an opportunity.

### 4. Sales process

Use consistent stages from discovery to closed outcome. Require a documented use case, stakeholder map, next step, and next-action date before advancing an opportunity.

## Illustrative 90-day targets

These targets would be validated against the real baseline after implementation:

- 95% of new inbound records include source and solution interest.
- 90% of MQLs receive a first sales action within the agreed SLA.
- Reduce unowned leads to below 2%.
- Establish baseline conversion and stage-velocity reporting.
- Improve MQL acceptance through two scoring-review cycles.

## Testing plan

1. Submit test leads for every source, product, geography, and consent state.
2. Confirm field mapping, deduplication, scoring, ownership, and notifications.
3. Test exclusions for customers, open opportunities, unsubscribes, and invalid emails.
4. Validate every workflow exit and error route.
5. Conduct user acceptance testing with marketing and sales.
6. Launch in a controlled phase and review daily for the first week.

## Optimization loop

Review accepted/rejected MQLs, stage conversion, response time, lost reasons, and won-customer patterns. Adjust one variable at a time—targeting, threshold, content, routing, or SLA—and document the change.
